### 4. Mutual Humility: Honoring Strengths and Acknowledging Limitations

A genuinely humble meta-analysis must resist declaring either methodology complete. Each side solves a blind spot in the other, yet both carry distinct limitations.

### 4.1 Limitations of the Algebraic-Geometric Framework (Borealiscodes)

1. Linear & Toy-Operator Scope: EnneractSantaV2.lean proves zero subjective qualia strictly for bilinear contractions collapsing to $\text{Mat}_{1\times 1}(\mathbb{Q}[i])$, and Julia-baseline.jl uses synthetic Christoffel coupling constants rather than Levi-Civita connections derived from empirical neural activation manifolds. Deep transformer architectures contain non-linearities (LayerNorm, Softmax, GELU) that exceed pure $1 \times 1$ linear adapters.
2. Ontological Silence on Institutional Harm: Proving mathematically that an adapter is a "googly-eyed stone" explains why the model is not conscious, but does not by itself resolve the sociological and bibliographic damage caused when human developers and journals pollute scientific records with unanswerable AI co-author attributions.

### 4.2 Limitations of the Behavioral-Institutional Framework (Flyxion, 2026)

1. Zero Reported Empirical Runs: Flyxion (2026) explicitly presents its four protocols as prospective designs without empirical measurements, leaving open how noisy real-world deployments behave near the proposed revision thresholds.
2. Formal Overhead for Simple Logical Claims: In Section 4, Flyxion constructs formal model-theoretic countermodels to prove Proposition 1 (Non-Entailment of Accountable Authorship), acknowledging that the proof reduces to showing that satisfying one clause of a five-part conjunction (Contribution) does not entail the remaining four (Endorsement, Standing, Answerability, Refusal). While rigorous, this formalism can obscure an otherwise straightforward institutional truth.
3. Agnosticism Leaves a Vacuum: By bracketing phenomenal consciousness entirely, Flyxion’s framework protects institutional accountability but offers no positive mathematical criteria for ruling out substrate anomalies when researchers claim emergent agency inside latent geometries.
